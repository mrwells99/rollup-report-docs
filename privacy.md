_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

Roll-up Report for Jira ("the app") is published by Lintra ("we").

## What the app does with your data
- The app reads Jira issues **as the signed-in user**, using that user's own Jira permissions. It reads only the fields it needs: summary, issue type, status, assignee name, parent, story points, original estimate, time spent and remaining estimate.
- It totals those values in Atlassian's Forge runtime and shows the result to the same user.
- **It doesn't store any Jira data.** It saves nothing to a database, Forge storage or files. The only thing saved is a dashboard gadget's own settings (the JQL and the grouping), which Jira stores on the dashboard.
- **It doesn't send data outside Atlassian.** The app makes no calls to external servers and qualifies for Atlassian's "Runs on Atlassian" program.
- It doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't use your data to train AI models.

## Personal data
Assignee display names are shown in reports while you view them and are never stored. Because the app stores no personal data, there's nothing for it to report or erase under GDPR or similar laws.

## Sub-processors
Atlassian (Forge hosting) is the only one. See [Atlassian's privacy policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy).
