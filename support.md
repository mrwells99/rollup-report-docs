# Roll-up Report for Jira: Support

Support is provided by Lintra.

- **Contact:** support@lintra.cloud
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday. We read mail on business days.
- **First response:** within 2 business days.
- **Security issues:** email the same address with "Security" in the subject; we acknowledge within 2 business days.

When you write, please include your Jira site name and what you were doing when the problem happened. Screenshots help.

See the [documentation](documentation.html) and [privacy policy](privacy.html).
